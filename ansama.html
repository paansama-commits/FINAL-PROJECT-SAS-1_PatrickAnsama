Part 1: Reflection of Self to Course Modules

The lessons from the different modules helped me understand that my sense of self is shaped by many interconnected factors. From the perspectives of self, I learned that who I am is not defined by a single idea but by a combination of reflection, behavior, and social interaction. This made me more aware of how I think about myself and how others influence that perception.

The module on biological influences helped me recognize that my habits, energy levels, and even emotional responses are partly shaped by my body and not just by choice. In terms of body image, I became more conscious of how I view my physical self, especially with my focus on fitness and nutrition. I now see my body not only in terms of appearance but also in terms of function and health.

The personality and identity development modules helped me understand why I tend to be introspective and reserved. I realized that I am still in the process of forming my identity, and that my decisions and experiences continue to shape who I am. Culture and ethnicity also play a strong role in my self-concept, especially through values such as respect, responsibility, and family.

The module on sexuality helped me become more aware of personal boundaries and relationships, while mental health and wellbeing emphasized the importance of balance, rest, and self-awareness. Overall, these lessons helped me see that my self is still developing, but I now have a clearer and more grounded understanding of who I am.

Part 2: Interview Summary

I interviewed two people who know me in different ways, my close friend from elementary and a classmate. Their answers were similar but also showed different sides of me.

My friend said that I am quiet at first, but I become more talkative once I feel comfortable. They described me as someone who prefers deep conversations instead of small talk. They also said I am thoughtful and often think a lot about things. According to them, I care about people but don’t always show it through words, but more through my actions.

My classmate described me as independent. They said I work well on my own and think carefully before I speak or act. At first, they thought I was distant, but over time they realized I am just reserved and selective about what I share. They also mentioned that I do my tasks seriously and can still cooperate well in group work.

Overall, both people described me as quiet, thoughtful, and dependable. They see me as someone who prefers meaningful interactions and takes time to open up. These interviews helped me understand how others see my personality and how my quietness can be seen in both positive and different ways.

Personal Manifesto

Personal Manifesto of Patrick Michelangelo A. Ansama

I believe that life is not something to simply go through, but something to consciously build. My life is guided by a commitment to growth, discipline, and self-awareness. I do not aim to be perfect—I aim to be intentional.

Core Values

I value discipline, growth, honesty, and balance. Discipline allows me to stay consistent even when motivation fades. Growth reminds me that I am always evolving. Honesty keeps me grounded in truth, and balance ensures that I do not lose myself in extremes.

Beliefs

I believe that people are capable of change, including myself. I believe that effort compounds over time, even when results are not immediately visible. I believe that discomfort is necessary for growth, and avoiding it leads to stagnation.

Virtues I Strive For

I strive to embody discipline, resilience, self-awareness, and integrity. Discipline keeps me steady, resilience helps me recover, self-awareness allows me to understand myself deeply, and integrity ensures that my actions align with my values.

Guiding Principles

  • Show up even when it’s difficult
  • Choose long-term growth over short-term comfort
  • Be honest with yourself before anything else
  • Control what you can, accept what you cannot
  • Stay consistent, even in small actions

Purpose

My purpose is to continuously develop myself—physically, mentally, and emotionally—so that I can live a meaningful life and contribute positively to others around me.

Intentions

I intend to live with awareness, to reflect regularly, and to act with purpose. I intend to remain disciplined in my habits and mindful of my thoughts and actions.

Goals

  • Maintain consistency in my health and fitness
  • Succeed in my academic journey and career path
  • Continue building habits that align with my long-term growth
  • Improve how I express myself and connect with others

Worldview

I see the world as a place that constantly challenges individuals to grow. People are shaped by their experiences, but they are not limited by them. I believe that meaning is not found—it is created through actions, choices, and persistence.
